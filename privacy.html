LaptopProof – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

LaptopProof is a Chrome extension that helps people check the condition of a laptop before buying it. This policy explains what the extension does with information.

Summary

LaptopProof does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network requests.

What the extension reads, and why

  • Hardware details (processor model, number of processor threads, installed memory, display information, graphics chip, operating system, battery level and charging state) are read so the person inspecting the laptop can compare them with the seller's listing.
  • Microphone and camera are used only when you press “Start microphone” or “Start camera”, and only to show a live level meter, a 5-second test recording you can play back, and a live camera preview. Recordings and video are never saved to disk or sent anywhere, and they are discarded when you leave that test or close the page.
  • Key presses, clicks and touchpad movement on the inspection page are used only to show which keys and gestures work.

What is stored

Your test results and notes are saved in the browser's local storage on the same laptop, so they survive a page reload. They never leave the device. Click “Start over” on the inspection page, or remove the extension, to delete them.

Sharing

A report only leaves the laptop if you choose to copy it or save it as a PDF and send it yourself.

Children

LaptopProof is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
