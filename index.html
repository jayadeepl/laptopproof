LaptopProof

Used laptop health check for Chrome

Buying a second-hand laptop? LaptopProof walks you through 10 quick tests on the laptop itself and catches the faults sellers don't mention, then gives you a report you can save as a PDF or share.

What it checks

  • Specs – the real processor, RAM, graphics and screen, to compare with the listing
  • Keyboard – every key lights up when it works
  • Screen – dead and stuck pixels, backlight bleed, refresh rate
  • Speakers, microphone and webcam
  • Touchpad – dead zones, clicks and scrolling
  • Battery – real wear level, cycle count and a drain test
  • Performance – catches overheating and throttling
  • Ports & body – a 12-point checklist, including company and iCloud locks

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. LaptopProof makes no network requests, and your results stay on the laptop you're testing. Read the privacy policy.
